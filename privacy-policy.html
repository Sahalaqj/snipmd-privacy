Privacy Policy — SnipMD

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Applies to the browser extension "SnipMD — Save Snippets to Markdown" (Microsoft Edge Add-ons).

Short version: SnipMD does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you save stays on your device.

1. What SnipMD does

SnipMD lets you select text on any web page and save it as a Markdown snippet together with its source (page title, URL, and save date). Snippets are managed from the extension's popup and can be copied or exported as .md files.

2. Data collection

SnipMD collects no personal data and no usage data:

  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • The extension makes no network requests — it works fully offline.

3. Data storage

All snippets are stored locally on your device only, using the browser's built-in local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). Your snippets are never uploaded to any server, and no one — including the developer — can access them. Clearing the extension's data or uninstalling the extension permanently removes all saved snippets.

4. Permissions

SnipMD requests the minimum permissions needed:

  • Storage — to save your snippets locally on your device.
  • Content script on web pages — to let you select text and save it with one click. The content script only reads the text you explicitly select and does not read or alter anything else on the page.

5. Third parties

SnipMD does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or external code, and shares no data with anyone.

6. Children's privacy

SnipMD does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no information at all.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and material changes will be reflected in the extension's store listing.
